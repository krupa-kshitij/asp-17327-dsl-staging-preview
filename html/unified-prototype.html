When to use which action

Reassociate MOVE

One-time correction or transfer. Product belongs to exactly one Organization at a time. Use when you need to fix a wrong association or move ownership.

"Product P1 is under O1 but should be under O2." Or "After M&A, all of NovaParts' products now belong to BoltLG Aerospace."

Manage Organizations LINK

Ongoing shared ownership. Product genuinely belongs to multiple Organizations and should appear in all their catalogs. Updates sync across all linked Orgs.

"Liebherr's hydraulic pump is sold by both Construction Equipment and Mining Equipment — both teams need it in their catalog."
